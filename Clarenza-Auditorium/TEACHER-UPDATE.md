# Coordinated teacher delivery

Implemented in the preserved auditorium:
- Face the class before presenting or narrating.
- Tap tablet, reveal the full slide, point, settle, then start narration.
- Keep the slide readable throughout speech; remove the conflicting typewriter overwrite.
- Insert six seconds for reading between automatic passages and allow roaming in silent intervals.
- Ease walking acceleration, use smaller turning steps and ease feet into their resting position.
- Return through visited aisle waypoints on entering focus and remain at the teaching area.
- Respect paused playback and the stationary toggle.

Validation covers a complete stair trip, narration/gesture sequencing, pausing pending narration, position stability while paused, focus return and staying home, and board stability. It does not establish production animation quality or validate a full five-minute lesson with human observers.

Remaining work: authored walk/turn/stair clips, better foot locking and tablet finger contact, semantic pointing to individual board elements, lip sync, listening/headset checks, and a learner-reviewed five-minute lesson. The upload/review/teach workflow is the next separate milestone; this release concentrates on classroom presentation.
